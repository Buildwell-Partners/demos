Nuestra Historia · Our Story

Run by sisters, built for the neighborhood

Carniceria Aurora is owned and run by sisters Maricela and Blanca Carillo. What started as a small carnicería on Havana Street has grown into a full market with a kitchen, still family-run and still serving the same neighbors.

Woman-owned · Hispanic-owned · Se habla español

  1. 2015

    The shop opens on Havana Street. Some longtime customers may remember it as La Consentida.

  2. Years at 354 Havana

    A 3,000-square-foot carnicería becomes a neighborhood go-to for meat and groceries.

  3. 2025 — A bigger home

    We moved down the block to 465 N Havana St into a space nearly three times the size, and added our own counter-service restaurant.
