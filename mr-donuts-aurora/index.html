About the shop

Small shop. Big Sunday line.

Mr. Donuts is an independent donut shop at 1704 S Chambers Rd in Aurora — not a chain. Neighbors know it for fresh donuts, filled donuts that are filled when you order them, and a line out the door on weekend mornings.
