What we fix

Repairs for the appliances your home runs on

Most major household appliances, diagnosed and repaired in your home by the owner himself.

Refrigerators

Not cooling, leaking, noisy or icing up — fridge and freezer troubleshooting and repair.

Washers

Won't drain, won't spin, leaking or throwing error codes.

Dryers

No heat, long dry times, squeaks and thumps, or not starting at all.

Ovens & ranges

Burners, bake and broil elements, igniters and temperature problems.

Dishwashers

Not cleaning, not draining, leaks and door or latch issues.

Garbage disposals

Jammed, humming, leaking or simply done — repaired or replaced.

Dryer vent cleaning

Clear lint build-up for faster drying and a safer laundry room.
