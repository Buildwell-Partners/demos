Trail Running & Hiking Shoes

Shoes with the grip, protection and fit for Douglas County single-track and high-country hikes.

RNK fits trail running shoes and hiking footwear at its Parker and Castle Rock stores for free, using the same gait-based fitting as for road shoes. We'll match the lug depth, rock protection, cushioning and fit to the terrain you run or hike, from Castle Rock's open spaces to Colorado 14ers.

Road, trail or hiking shoe?

  • Road shoes suit pavement and smooth crushed-gravel paths like the Cherry Creek Trail.
  • Trail running shoes add deeper lugs, a tougher upper and often a rock plate for rocky, rooty or loose ground.
  • Hiking shoes and boots trade some flexibility for stability and protection when you're carrying a pack or covering long, rough descents.

Fitting tips for Colorado trails

  • Leave a thumb's width of room in front of your longest toe; descents push your foot forward.
  • Look for a secure heel and midfoot so the shoe doesn't shift on off-camber trail.
  • Wear the socks you'll hike or run in, and bring them to the fitting.
