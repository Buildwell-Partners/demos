Running Store FAQ

Fittings, returns, hours, group runs and which store is closest — answered.

Do you do free gait analysis in Parker and Castle Rock?

Yes. Every shoe fitting at both RNK stores includes a gait and foot assessment at no charge: we look at your feet, watch you walk and jog, and use that to narrow down shoes that suit how you move. No appointment is needed. See how a fitting works.

How long does a shoe fitting take, and do I need an appointment?

Plan on about 30–45 minutes for a full running or walking shoe fitting. Appointments aren't required at either store. Weekday afternoons are usually the quietest; Saturday mornings are the busiest.

What should I bring to a running shoe fitting?

Bring the shoes you currently run or walk in (wear pattern tells us a lot), the socks you usually wear, and any orthotics or inserts you use. If you're training for a race or dealing with a sore spot, tell your fitter up front.

What is your return policy if the shoes don't work out?

Every pair is covered by the RNK Happiness Guarantee. If shoes you bought from us aren't working, even after you've run in them, bring them back to either store and we'll work with you to find a pair that does. Ask your fitter for the details that apply to your purchase.

Do you fit walking shoes for plantar fasciitis, bunions or Morton's neuroma?

Yes, we regularly fit walkers and runners dealing with heel pain, bunions and forefoot pain. We're shoe fitters, not doctors: we'll find comfortable, supportive options with the right shape and cushioning, and we're glad to work alongside your podiatrist or physical therapist. See walking shoes & foot pain.

How often should I replace running shoes?

Most running shoes last roughly 300–500 miles. Signs it's time: the midsole looks creased and compressed, the outsole is worn through in spots, or familiar aches return. Bring your old pair in and we'll take a look for free.

Do you carry wide sizes and wide toe-box shoes?

Yes. Many models we carry come in wide (and some in extra-wide) widths, and brands such as Altra and Topo are built with a naturally roomy toe box. Stock varies by store, so call ahead if you need a specific width.

Do you fit sports bras, and how long does it take?

Yes. A sports bra fitting takes about 15–20 minutes in a private fitting room, and it's free. See sports bra fitting.

Are your group runs free, and can beginners join?

All RNK group runs are free and open to every pace. The Thursday Chog in Castle Rock is a conversational-pace run, and the Second Saturday run in Parker has a beginner and stroller-friendly group. See the group run schedule.

What are your store hours on Sunday and holidays?

Both stores are open Sunday 11 am – 5 pm. Monday–Friday hours are 11 am – 7 pm and Saturday is 10 am – 6 pm. Holiday hours are posted on each store's Google listing and our Facebook page.

Can I order a specific shoe or color I don't see in store?

Usually, yes. Ask any fitter and we'll check with the brand, or browse our online shop for in-store pickup at either location.

Which store is closer to Lone Tree, Elizabeth or Castle Pines?

From Lone Tree and Elizabeth, the Parker store at 13019 S Parker Rd is usually the closer drive. From Castle Pines, Larkspur and Sedalia, the Castle Rock store on 4th Street in downtown Castle Rock is usually closer.
