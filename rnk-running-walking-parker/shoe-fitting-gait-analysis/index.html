Free Running Shoe Fitting & Gait Analysis

A free, no-appointment fitting at either store: we measure your feet, watch how you move and help you test a short list of shoes chosen for you.

RNK's shoe fitting is free, takes about 30–45 minutes and needs no appointment at our Parker or Castle Rock store. A fitter measures both feet, watches you walk and jog to assess your gait, asks about your running or walking goals, then has you try and test several shoes before you choose.

What happens during a fitting

  1. Conversation. How much you run or walk, on what surfaces, any races on the calendar, and any aches or past injuries.
  2. Foot measurement. Length, width and arch length on both feet, standing. Most people's feet change size over the years, and many of us are wearing the wrong size.
  3. Gait assessment. We watch you walk and jog to see how your foot lands and rolls, and look at the wear pattern on the shoes you came in with.
  4. Guided try-on. Your fitter brings out three to five shoes chosen for your foot shape and stride, across several brands.
  5. Test it. Jog outside or around the store in each pair. The shoe you choose should disappear on your foot.
  6. Finishing touches. Socks, insoles or lacing tweaks if they help, and advice on how many miles to expect from the pair.

What to bring

  • The shoes you currently run or walk in
  • The socks you usually wear
  • Any orthotics or inserts
  • Your training plan or race date, if you have one

How long it takes and what it costs

The fitting is free, with no obligation to buy. Plan on 30–45 minutes; a quick re-buy of a shoe you already love takes ten. Weekday afternoons are the quietest time to come in.

Why it matters

The right shoe is the one that matches your foot's shape and the way you move, not the one at the top of a review list. Getting that right is the cheapest injury prevention there is, and it's why every pair from RNK is covered by our Happiness Guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do free gait analysis in Parker and Castle Rock?

Yes. Every shoe fitting at both RNK stores includes a gait and foot assessment at no charge: we look at your feet, watch you walk and jog, and use that to narrow down shoes that suit how you move. No appointment is needed. See how a fitting works.

How long does a shoe fitting take, and do I need an appointment?

Plan on about 30–45 minutes for a full running or walking shoe fitting. Appointments aren't required at either store. Weekday afternoons are usually the quietest; Saturday mornings are the busiest.

What should I bring to a running shoe fitting?

Bring the shoes you currently run or walk in (wear pattern tells us a lot), the socks you usually wear, and any orthotics or inserts you use. If you're training for a race or dealing with a sore spot, tell your fitter up front.

How often should I replace running shoes?

Most running shoes last roughly 300–500 miles. Signs it's time: the midsole looks creased and compressed, the outsole is worn through in spots, or familiar aches return. Bring your old pair in and we'll take a look for free.
