About us

An Iraqi-owned taste of home on Havana Street

Ali Restaurant & Bakery — also known as Ali International Market — is an Iraqi-owned halal restaurant, bakery and market in Aurora's Havana Street international food corridor. Everything we serve is halal, and no alcohol is sold or served.

We're growing: the market is expanding into the space next door so the restaurant has more room to seat you.
