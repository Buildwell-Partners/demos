About

A local team that treats your home like a showroom.

Flooring Trends is a floor and wall remodeling company based in Littleton, Colorado, led by Samuel Oliveira. The team works on residential and commercial projects across the Denver metro.

Whether you're updating one room or redoing an entire level, you get a crew that installs every major flooring type — so you can choose the right material for each space without juggling contractors.
