Our story

Named for family

TNT is a family business, owned by Laticia Lewis and named for her children — Terrell, Naynay and Tyrese. It was recognized by CBS Colorado as one of the first Black-owned pizza shops in Colorado.

As the family put it to CBS, the restaurant is meant to be "just a home for everyone."

Giving back in ParkerThe family is known for sharing pizza with people experiencing homelessness, first responders and foster families.
