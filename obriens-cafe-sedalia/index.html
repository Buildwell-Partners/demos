Our story

A little taste of home, since 1994

O'Brien's Cafe is run by co-owners Donna Ingman and Arlene O'Brien. Donna opened the cafe in 1994, and for three decades it has been a hometown gathering spot on US-85.

The baking was so popular that the same family later opened Sedalia Bakery just down the road — so you know the cinnamon rolls and pies are the real deal.

  • 1994Donna Ingman opens O'Brien's Cafe on Highway 85 in Sedalia.
  • Every morningDoors open at 5 AM for coffee, burritos and breakfast plates.
  • Sedalia BakeryDemand for the baked goods leads the owners to open a scratch bakery in town.
  • TodayStill family-run, still homemade, still the hometown breakfast stop.
