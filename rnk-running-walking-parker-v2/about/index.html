About RNK Running & Walking

A locally owned Douglas County running store, co-owned by an Olympic Trials marathoner.

RNK Running & Walking is a locally owned specialty running, walking and hiking store with locations in Parker and Castle Rock, Colorado, co-owned by Trent Briney and Jim Browning. Trent qualified for the 2004 U.S. Olympic Team Trials in the marathon.

Our approach

Big-box stores sell shoes; we fit them. Every visit starts with a conversation and a look at how you move, because a shoe that works for your training partner may be wrong for you. As Trent puts it, "We want to assume you're you."

Our Parker store on S Parker Rd celebrated its grand re-opening with the Parker Chamber in 2017, and our downtown Castle Rock store on 4th Street now serves runners and walkers across the south end of Douglas County.

Trent Briney

A Colorado native, Trent learned to run on the trails around Garden of the Gods in Colorado Springs. At the University of Colorado Colorado Springs he was a four-time NCAA Division II All-American. He later trained with the Hansons-Brooks distance project, where his 2:21:10 debut at the 2003 Chicago Marathon qualified him for the 2004 U.S. Olympic Team Trials in the men's marathon. He went on to finish as second man at the 2012 JFK 50 Mile, one of the oldest ultramarathons in the country.

Today he brings that experience to fittings, training advice and the free group runs that leave from both stores each week.

In the community

We host free weekly group runs, a free fall marathon training run, and brand demo runs, and we sponsor local races such as Parker Parks and Recreation's 5K and 10K series.
