Walking Shoes for Comfort & Foot Pain

Comfort-first fittings for walkers, people on their feet all day, and anyone whose feet hurt.

RNK fits walking shoes for everyday comfort, long work shifts and common foot complaints like heel pain, bunions and wide feet. The fitting is free and takes about 30–45 minutes at our Parker or Castle Rock store. We're shoe fitters rather than doctors, and we're happy to work with your podiatrist or physical therapist.

Who we fit

  • Daily walkers and fitness walkers
  • Nurses, teachers, retail and warehouse staff who stand for long shifts
  • People with heel pain, plantar fasciitis, bunions or forefoot pain
  • Wide feet, high-volume feet and people who wear orthotics

How we approach foot pain

We look for shoes with the right shape for your foot first: enough room across the forefoot and toes, a heel that holds without rubbing, then the cushioning and stability that make standing and walking comfortable. Brands such as HOKA, Brooks, Altra and Topo offer wide widths and roomy toe boxes, and insoles can add arch support where it helps.

We don't diagnose or treat foot conditions. If you have a prescription orthotic or a podiatrist's recommendation, bring it and we'll fit around it.

Fitting process

It's the same free process as our running shoe fitting: measure, watch you walk, try several options and walk in each one. No appointment needed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you fit walking shoes for plantar fasciitis, bunions or Morton's neuroma?

Yes, we regularly fit walkers and runners dealing with heel pain, bunions and forefoot pain. We're shoe fitters, not doctors: we'll find comfortable, supportive options with the right shape and cushioning, and we're glad to work alongside your podiatrist or physical therapist. See walking shoes & foot pain.

Do you carry wide sizes and wide toe-box shoes?

Yes. Many models we carry come in wide (and some in extra-wide) widths, and brands such as Altra and Topo are built with a naturally roomy toe box. Stock varies by store, so call ahead if you need a specific width.

What is your return policy if the shoes don't work out?

Every pair is covered by the RNK Happiness Guarantee. If shoes you bought from us aren't working, even after you've run in them, bring them back to either store and we'll work with you to find a pair that does. Ask your fitter for the details that apply to your purchase.
