Why Parker chooses Goldstar

A local shop with a near-perfect rating

Goldstar Cleaners has been part of the Ponderosa Drive shopping strip for about two decades, and under ownership that took over in October 2024 it has kept earning the kind of reviews most businesses only hope for: a 4.9-star average across 84 Google reviews.

Customers keep coming back for the same reasons — clothes that come back looking great, prices that feel fair, and people at the counter who are genuinely nice to deal with.

  • Independent & local — not a franchise; your neighbors run the shop.
  • Early weekday hours — open at 7 AM Monday through Friday.
  • Saturday service — handle your dry cleaning on the weekend too.
