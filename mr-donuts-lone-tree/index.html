About us

A family donut shop with a following

Mr. Donuts is a family-run, small-batch donut shop in Lone Tree. Neighbors line up early for donuts that are light and airy, creative flavors, and fillings that don't hold back — and the kolaches have their own fan club. Local food writers have noticed too: the shop has been featured by OnlyInYourState.
