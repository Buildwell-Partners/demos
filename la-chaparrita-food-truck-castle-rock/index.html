About

Small truck, big flavor

La Chaparrita is a small, family-owned Mexican food truck run by owner Laura Mendez Ceron and a proud member of the Castle Rock Chamber of Commerce.

The idea is simple: real Mexican flavors, cooked fresh to order, with portions and sides that don’t skimp.
