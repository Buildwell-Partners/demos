Questions, answered

Let's talk shoes.

Is RNK's shoe fitting free?

Yes. RNK's current service information says shoe fitting and gait analysis are free, and no appointment is needed. Visit either store to get started.

What happens during a running shoe fitting?

The team discusses your history and goals, assesses your movement and foot measurements, brings out suitable shoes and uses treadmill video to help compare options.

What should I bring to a shoe fitting?

Bring the socks you normally use, your current shoes and any insoles or orthotics you wear. Your old shoes can help the fitter understand what you've been using.

Can I shop online and pick up in store?

RNK's online shop displays a pickup-in-store filter. Confirm availability and pickup details during checkout.

What is the return policy?

RNK's online shop currently advertises a 30-day return policy. Review the full terms on the shop before purchasing.
