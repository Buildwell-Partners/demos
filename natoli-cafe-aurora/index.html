Favorites at Natoli

A look at what diners come back for. Ask at the counter for today's full selection.

Injera platters

Generous platters served on soft, tangy injera — the heart of the Ethiopian table.

Awaze tibs

Sautéed beef or lamb in spicy awaze — the dish reviewers mention by name again and again.

Sambusa

Crisp, golden pastry triangles — a perfect starter or snack with coffee.

Chapati

Soft, flaky flatbread, great alongside stews and tibs.

Coffee & chai

Fresh-ground Ethiopian coffee and chai — see below.

Baklava

A sweet finish — layered pastry and honey with your coffee or chai.
