Quick answer

Majestic Exteriors replaces and repairs residential and commercial roofs across Greater Denver in asphalt shingle, metal, tile, wood shake and copper. Inspections and written estimates are free, and most single-family replacements take one to two days.

Shingle Roofing

Asphalt shingles come in many colors, grades and styles, backed with fiberglass or organic material. They are the most common roofing material in the United States. In hail country, ask about Class 4 impact-rated lines, which some insurers discount.

Metal Roofing

Available in panels and shingle profiles. Metal roofs are lightweight, shed snow and stand up well to severe weather and high wind.

Tile Roofing

Concrete and clay tiles come in many colors and finishes. Tile looks great and lasts for decades, but it costs more and is heavy, so we check the structure first.

Wood Shingle or Shake Shingle

Wood shingles and shakes are made from cedar, redwood, pine and other woods. Shingles are machine cut; shakes are hand split.

Copper Roofing

Durable, long-lasting and striking. Copper is common on high-end and historic homes, bay windows, cupolas and accents.

Commercial Roofing

Single-ply and modified bitumen systems for high-rise condominiums, retail and industrial buildings. We are an approved applicator for many leading insurance carriers. References on request.

FAQ

Roofing questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family asphalt shingle roofs are replaced in one to two days once materials arrive, weather permitting. Larger, steeper, tile, metal and commercial roofs take longer. You get a written schedule with your estimate.

How much does a new roof cost in the Denver area?

Price depends on roof size, pitch, layers to tear off, and material. Asphalt shingles cost least, then metal, tile, wood shake and copper. Majestic Exteriors gives free written estimates, and when hail caused the damage your insurer usually pays most of the cost.

What is a Class 4 impact-resistant roof?

Class 4 is the highest rating under the UL 2218 impact test, in which steel balls up to 2 inches are dropped on the roofing. Class 4 shingles hold up better to Colorado hail, and some insurers offer a premium discount for them. Ask your insurer whether yours does.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. Majestic Exteriors repairs and installs roofs on high-rise condominiums, industrial, retail and commercial buildings as well as single-family homes, and is an approved applicator for many leading insurance carriers. References are available on request.
