Quick answer

Majestic Exteriors installs and services aluminum and steel gutters and downspouts. Aluminum is the most popular choice for its cost and rust resistance; steel is stronger for heavy snow and rain.

Gutters

Aluminum is the most popular gutter on the market for its low cost and durability. It stays rust-free and comes in a broad range of colors. Steel gutters are usually galvanized, with stainless steel as a rust-proof premium option. Galvanized steel can rust over time but is highly resilient in extreme weather, heavy rain and snow, which makes it worth the higher price for many Colorado homes.

Gutters and ice dams

Freeze and thaw cycles on the Front Range can build ice dams at the eaves. Properly sized gutters, clear downspouts and good attic ventilation help prevent them. If you have one now, call. Diane M. had us on site within an hour.
