Quick answer

Majestic Exteriors replaces and repairs roofs, siding and gutters in Littleton from our shop in neighboring Englewood. We offer free hail inspections, meet your adjuster and pull the permit for you.

Littleton sits just south of our Englewood shop, so we can usually inspect within 48 hours of your call. Because Littleton spans Arapahoe, Jefferson and Douglas counties, the permitting office depends on your address. We handle that for you.

Common Littleton roofing jobs

  • Hail replacements on 1970s to 1990s ranch and two-story homes.
  • Class 4 upgrades for homeowners tired of filing a claim every few years.
  • Gutter and siding repairs after the same storm.
