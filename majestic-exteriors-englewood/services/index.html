Quick answer

Majestic Exteriors offers roof replacement and repair (asphalt shingle, metal, tile, wood shake, copper and commercial systems), hail damage and insurance claim help, siding (vinyl, aluminum, James Hardie, T1-11) and aluminum or steel gutters across Greater Denver. Estimates are free.

Majestic Exteriors has repaired and installed thousands of roofs across the Denver metro area, from single-family homes to high-rise condominiums, industrial, retail and commercial buildings. Requests for references are welcome. We are an approved applicator for many leading insurance carriers, from roof shingles to single-ply modified roofing systems.
