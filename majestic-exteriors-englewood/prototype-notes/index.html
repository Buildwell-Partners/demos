Prototype Notes

Home/Prototype notes

This version keeps the look of today’s majesticexteriors.net and rebuilds it underneath, using the SEO, AEO and GEO approach of buildwell.partners. Yellow-taped text is a placeholder to confirm with Jake. Not part of the live site.

Kept from majesticexteriors.net

  • Layout: red top bar with phone, email and address; white header that shrinks as you scroll; full-width photo slider; the red “Request Free Quote” card overlapping the hero; the dark “Who We Are” band; centered “Services” heading with red rules; the house illustration over the red shingle testimonial band; zig-zag service rows; white footer with Contact Info and Quick Links over a gray copyright bar.
  • Fonts and color: DM Serif Text headings, Poppins body text, Majestic red (#EA3026), square red buttons, red bars beside headings.
  • Photos: every image comes from the current site (both slider photos, the sunset roofer, the house illustration, the shingle texture and the service photos), converted to WebP.
  • Motion: the slider crossfade and slide-in arrows, fade-in headlines, the services dropdown, the nav hover fade and entrance animations as sections scroll into view. All motion switches off for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion.
  • Addresses: /services/, /about-us/ and /contact/ stay where they are, and old links such as /services/#copper still land on the right section.

Built in, matching buildwell.partners

Each item below is something buildwell.partners does on its own site, applied to Majestic.

  • Real pages at real URLs. Each service, city and the contact page has its own address, title, description and canonical tag.
  • One linked schema graph. The business, website and owner are described in a single JSON-LD graph and point to each other by stable @id, and every inner page adds a WebPage with breadcrumbs that points back to the business.
  • Business details search engines and AI assistants can read: the service catalog, areas served, audiences, topics the company knows about and its founder.
  • An FAQ block that matches the visible questions word for word.
  • Cited statistics: a named source under every number in the fact strip.
  • Crawler files: llms.txt, robots.txt pointing to the sitemap, sitemap.xml and an HTML sitemap.
  • Search, local and sharing tags: unique descriptions, canonical links, geo.region and geo.placename, Open Graph and Twitter card tags.

Added for Majestic

  • Answer-first “Quick answer” blocks near the top of each page, written to be quoted by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity.
  • Step-by-step claim guide in HowTo schema, matching the hail claim steps on the page.
  • Fast by default: no WordPress, Divi or jQuery. One small stylesheet, one small script, WebP photos with set sizes, lazy loading below the fold.

Guesses to confirm with Jake

  • Google rating and count, license numbers, insurance, manufacturer certifications, warranty, financing partner, hours, founding year and social profiles.

This preview vs. launch

This preview is marked noindex so it never competes with majesticexteriors.net. At launch the same pages go live on the real domain with robots set to index, follow, max-snippet:-1, max-image-preview:large, a Google Business Profile that matches the name, address and phone exactly, and call and form tracking.
