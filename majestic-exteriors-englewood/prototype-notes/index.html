Prototype notes · not part of the live site

What changed and why

This prototype is built on the same playbook as buildwell.partners: the same SEO, AEO and GEO markup, typefaces and page layout, with Majestic's red as the action color. Yellow-taped text is a placeholder to confirm with Jake.

Red flags on the current site

IssueWhy it mattersFixed here by
Every page says noindex, nofollowThe site tells Google not to list it. It cannot rank for anything, including "Majestic Exteriors".Indexable pages with max-snippet and large image previews allowed, robots.txt, XML and HTML sitemaps
No structured data, sitemap or meta descriptionsSearch engines and AI assistants get no machine-readable facts about the business.A linked schema graph on every page, plus unique titles, descriptions, canonicals and Open Graph tags

Built in, matching buildwell.partners

  • Real pages at real URLs. Each service, city and the contact page has its own address, title, description, canonical tag and Open Graph card, the way buildwell.partners has /industries/, /pricing/ and /contact/.
  • One linked schema graph. The home page carries the business (RoofingContractor) with slogan, service catalog, service area, audiences, topics it knows about and a "book an inspection" action, plus WebSite and WebPage. Every other page adds a WebPage with breadcrumbs that points back to the business by its @id.
  • Visible FAQs that match the schema word for word, in the same two-column layout, so Google and AI assistants can quote them.
  • Cited statistics. The fact strip names a source under every number, like the Invoca and Harvard Business Review figures on buildwell.partners.
  • Answer-first "Quick answer" blocks at the top of each inner page.
  • Local signals: geo.region and geo.placename tags, an areaServed list and a dedicated city page.
  • Crawler files: llms.txt, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and an HTML sitemap.
  • The same look: Fraunces headlines, Inter body text, navy and cream grounds, pill buttons, the Back / Book / Next bar at the top and bottom of every page, and a navy footer with a logo card.

Guesses to confirm with Jake

  • ZIP 80110, the 18-city service list and "most replacements take 1 to 2 days".
  • Google rating and count, license numbers, insurance, manufacturer certifications, warranty, financing partner, hours, founding year and social profiles.
  • Kristi, Damon and Dan's roles, which are inferred from reviews.
  • Material lifespans are general industry ranges, not Majestic warranties.

This preview vs. launch

This preview is marked noindex so it never competes with majesticexteriors.net. At launch the same pages go live on the real domain with robots set to index, follow, max-snippet:-1, max-image-preview:large, a Google Business Profile that matches the name, address and phone exactly, and call and form tracking.
