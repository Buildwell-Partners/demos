Siding

Siding installation in Greater Denver

Quick answer

Majestic Exteriors installs vinyl, aluminum, James Hardie fiber cement and T1-11 siding across Greater Denver, often together with a roof or gutter project after a hailstorm.

Vinyl

No painting. Cleans with soap and water. Wide range of colors and styles at the lowest cost.

Aluminum

Strong and long-lasting, often 40 years or more. Takes paint well, though it can dent.

James Hardie fiber cement

30 to 50 year manufacturer warranty, highly fire-resistant. Repaint and caulk every 5 to 10 years.

T1-11

Plywood or OSB panels. Stain for a natural wood look or prime and paint. Plywood lasts longer.

Does hail damage siding?

Yes. Vinyl can crack and aluminum dents, especially on the side facing the storm. We check siding, window wraps and screens on every hail inspection so it all goes into one claim instead of two.
