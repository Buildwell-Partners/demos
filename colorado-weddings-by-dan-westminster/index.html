About

Meet Dan Gustad

Colorado Weddings by Dan is the officiant service of Dan Gustad, a fully licensed wedding officiant based in the Denver area. Dan performs both secular and spiritual ceremonies and travels to couples all over Colorado.

Whether you are planning a big celebration in the metro or a quiet elopement in the high country, Dan works with you on a ceremony that sounds like you — not like a script.
