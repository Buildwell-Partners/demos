Our story

A "For Rent" sign, an old gas station, and 45 years

In 1980 Gary Bryan spotted a "For Rent" sign on an old gas station at 44th and Teller and signed the lease. More than four decades later the shop is still family-run — Gary works alongside his wife, Mimi — and it has never needed to advertise. Customers simply tell their friends.

In July 2025 Wheat Ridge Auto Service marked 45 years in business, and the local paper stopped by to celebrate.
