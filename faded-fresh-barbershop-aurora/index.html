What we do

The Menu

Every barber on the team cuts all types of hair. Not sure what you want? Show us a picture and we'll take it from there.

Fades & Tapers

Skin, low, mid and high fades with clean, gradual blends. This is what the shop is known for.

Designs & Parts

Hair designs, hard parts and line work, done freehand by barbers who specialize in it.

Classic & Modern Cuts

Scissor cuts, crops, tapers and longer styles. We cut every style for every hair type.

Every Hair Type

Straight, wavy, curly or coily. The team cuts all hair types and textures, any style.

Kids' Cuts

Affordable cuts for all ages. Bring the whole family in.

Walk-Ins Welcome

With ten barbers on the team, walk-ins are welcome Monday through Saturday, 9am–7pm.

Meet the owner

Built by a barber, Ruben Javier

Ruben started cutting his own hair as a young teen. Before long he was cutting hair for neighbors and classmates, and he went on to open his own shop and a barber school.

A road trip brought him to Denver, and he expanded the business here. Faded Fresh now sits on the Southeast Denver / Aurora border, and its ten barbers are all specialists in every type of haircut, design and style.

He has called the journey one with ups and downs. The focus hasn't changed: quality cuts, a chill atmosphere and fair prices for everyone who walks in.
