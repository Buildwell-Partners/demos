Our story

A Centennial tradition for over 30 years

Joyce Marie's Tailoring & Alterations has served the south Denver metro since 1990 from its shop on East Dry Creek Road. Owner Lisa Anderson and the team handle everything from a simple hem to a full bridal fitting.

Customers on Yelp point to the same things again and again: fair prices, quick turnaround, and a team that will go the extra mile to finish a rush order in time for an event.
