About us

Small, family-run & welcoming

Charrito's House is a family-run Mexican restaurant in Larkspur, cooking from family recipes for breakfast, lunch and dinner. Guests describe it as small, lively and friendly, with reasonable prices and plates that leave you full.

We're a short drive from the Jellystone Park campground and right on Spruce Mountain Road — easy to reach from Castle Rock, Monument and Palmer Lake.
