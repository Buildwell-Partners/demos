Our Shop

A barbershop the way it used to be

Since 2016, The Owl's Nest has been Castle Rock's spot for a traditional barbershop experience. The shop has a vintage feel, the pace is unhurried, and the focus is on doing the craft well.

Owner and master barber Ben Grulke runs the chair. The work runs from tailored haircuts to hot lather straight shaves, straight razor fades and custom designs. It's the kind of place men come back to for a high-quality cut and a real grooming experience.
