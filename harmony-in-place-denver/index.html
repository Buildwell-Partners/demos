About

Meet Lucas Macias

Harmony In Place is the organizing business of Lucas Macias, who has been helping Denver households get organized for five years. Lucas works hands-on in clients’ homes — from a single closet to a full move — and has been hired 87 times through Thumbtack alone.

The goal is simple: spaces that feel calm, are easy to keep up, and fit the way you live.
