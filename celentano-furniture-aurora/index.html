What we do

Upholstery and restoration, done by hand

From a favorite armchair to a full sofa, we give good furniture another life instead of sending it to the curb.

Reupholstery

New fabric for sofas, chairs and more, fitted and finished on your existing frame.

New cushions

Flat or worn-out cushions rebuilt or replaced so your seating feels right again.

Custom slipcovers

Made-to-measure slipcovers that refresh a piece and protect it from everyday wear.

Wood restoration

Restoring the wood on furniture pieces so the frame looks as good as the fabric.

Also: upholstery repairs, and specialty upholstery like gym equipment pads. Not sure if your piece is worth redoing? Send a photo and ask.

About us

Meet Michael & Emily

Celentano Furniture is a husband-and-wife business run by co-owners Michael and Emily Celentano. Since about 2022 we've been reupholstering, re-cushioning and restoring furniture for homeowners around Aurora and Denver.

As a family-run shop, you talk directly with the owners, from the first estimate to the finished piece.
